# 1/24/2026 4 PM - Battery charging - change in plan  

_Time spent: 1.5h_  

I just realized that i should look up the components in lcsc electronics to see if it even has the components that i need. 

As i mentioned before, i wanted to use the MAX1898EUB41 to charge the battery, since it can give feedback to the esp32, and tell how much battery is left. However, i checked lcsc electronics and they only have 5 pieces left, therefore 1 piece cost around 6 USD which is A LOT. 

I therefore had to change my plan to another chip, and i used a simpler chip, MCP73831, which actually is simpler than the one before. I used the formula in the data sheet to calculate how many mA should be drawn by the battery, and i decided that it should be around 330mA for better protection, by choosing a 3Kohm resistor between prog & GND.
